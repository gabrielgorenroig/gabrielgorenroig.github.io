---
title: Emergence and Information
---

I am interested in developing a mathematical yet qualitative understanding of emergence ("the whole being more than the sum of the parts"), in contrast with-but also complementary to-predominant quantitative approaches based on information theory. I believe category theory is key in achieving this since it gives the appropriate language to talk about the composition algebra of interconnected systems. From this perspective, emergence stands tightly connected to the concept of compositionality.

I am also interested in qualitative notions of _information content_, either in terms of minimal description length (such as Kolmogorov complexity a.k.a. algorithmic information, which featured prominently in [my Licentiate thesis](/assets/pdf/Goren_Gabriel_Tesis_Lic.pdf){:target="_blank"}), or in terms of coarse-graining: "information is that which is lost through coarse-graining". This perspective contributes to my interest in model theory: the equivalence classes given by logical indistinguishability of models constitute a form of logical or language-based coarse-graining.
