My interests tend to be somewhat interdisciplinary; this should not come as a surprise if you know that I hold a Licentiate degree in physics (~Master's degree in Argentina), my advisor is a computer scientist, and I do my PhD (and teaching) in the mathematics department. In academia, as elsewhere, there is a natural tension between tradition and innovation. I believe there is no knowledge without tradition, but we would do well in revising some widely held distinctions between mathematical disciplines. In particular, I am suspicious of the conceptual separation between "pure" and "applied" mathematics. I think this is well reflected in my interests below.
