Hi there! I'm Gabo, a PhD student in Mathematics at [Universidad de Buenos Aires](https://www.uba.ar/) in Buenos Aires, Argentina, working at the intersection of category theory, logic and computer science. My interests include categorical logic, computational category theory, applied category theory and complex systems.

My research is funded by [CONICET](https://www.conicet.gov.ar/), Argentina's National Research Council. You can find my list of publications [here](/publications/).
